## Production tools

Python, NumPy, Pillow and OpenCV prepared and checked the textures. Qt and PyQt5 created the offscreen OpenGL context. GLSL composited each frame, FFmpeg and NVIDIA NVENC encoded the MP4, and ffprobe inspected its streams and timestamps. Microsoft Consolas supplied the raster lettering. Segoe UI was used on the private comparison sheet. The font files are supplied by the local Windows installation. Playwright and Chrome checked the playback wrap. Lively Wallpaper is the intended desktop player.

[Python](https://www.python.org/) · [NumPy](https://numpy.org/) · [Pillow](https://python-pillow.org/) · [OpenCV](https://opencv.org/) · [Qt](https://www.qt.io/) · [PyQt5](https://www.riverbankcomputing.com/software/pyqt/intro) · [OpenGL](https://www.opengl.org/) · [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org/) · [NVIDIA Video Codec SDK](https://developer.nvidia.com/video-codec-sdk) · [Consolas](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/typography/font-list/consolas) · [Playwright](https://playwright.dev/) · [Lively Wallpaper](https://github.com/lively-community/lively)
